# Tamil translation guide (தமிழ் மொழிபெயர்ப்பு வழிகாட்டி)

Audience: Tamil-speaking Christians and students, Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka. Register: clear, formal, modern
written Tamil (பொதுத் தமிழ்), as in good Tamil Christian study books — not archaic, not colloquial.
Biblical vocabulary follows the Tamil Bible tradition (Tamil Union / BSI "O.V."): தேவன், கர்த்தர், இயேசு கிறிஸ்து.

## Hard rules
1. Translate meaning faithfully. Do not add, drop, soften or sharpen any claim. This app is even-handed between views; keep it so.
2. Keep placeholders exactly: `{n}`, `{total}`, `{name}` etc. stay in Latin letters, unchanged.
3. Keep all numbers, verse numbers and dates as digits (Revelation 13:18 → வெளிப்படுத்தின விசேஷம் 13:18; AD 70 → கி.பி. 70; 586 BC → கி.மு. 586).
4. Bible book names inside sentences: use the Tamil names below. Keep chapter:verse digits.
5. Scholars' and authors' names, and titles of books and articles, stay in English letters (e.g. Kenneth Gentry, *Before Jerusalem Fell*). Ancient writers may be given in Tamil with no English (ஜோசீபஸ், யூசிபியஸ், ஐரேனியஸ், தாசித்தஸ்).
6. Greek, Hebrew and Latin words that the English keeps (apokalypsis, parousia, genea, dominus et deus) stay in Latin letters; you may add a Tamil gloss in brackets if the English gives one.
7. On first use of a technical school name you may add the English in brackets, e.g. "பின்-ஆயிரவருடக் கொள்கை (Postmillennialism)". Short UI labels: Tamil only.
8. Short interface strings (buttons, tabs): short Tamil, natural for an app.
9. Keep quotation marks and punctuation style; straight or curly quotes as in the English.
10. Output must be valid JSON. Escape double quotes inside values as \".
